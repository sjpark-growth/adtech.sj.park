How I work

  1. Diagnose — budget flow, brand-keyword dependency and efficiency by placement. Find where spend is leaking first.
  2. Agree on goals — ROAS and revenue targets plus the guardrails to hold, e.g. a ROAS floor or a daily budget.
  3. Run performance — rebuild campaign structure; adjust bids, budgets and products daily. Every change is logged.
  4. Keywords · SEO — widen the keywords ads can win, and fix product names, tags, attributes and categories so products rank without ads.
  5. Report · scale — one weekly set of numbers in the performance data dashboard; budget grows only where efficiency is proven.
